Data Deletion Instructions for FrauduLens

Last updated: January 2025

How to Delete Your Account and Data

Method 1: Delete via App (Recommended)

  1. Open the FrauduLens mobile app
  2. Navigate to Settings (usually in Profile or Menu)
  3. Tap on "Account Settings" or "Delete Account"
  4. Read the deletion warning carefully
  5. Enter your password to confirm (if required)
  6. Tap "Delete Account" or "Confirm Deletion"
  7. Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted

Method 2: Request Deletion via Email

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by email:

  1. Send an email to: support@fraudulens.com
  2. Subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
  3. Include the following information:
    • Your registered email address
    • Your username (if applicable)
    • Reason for deletion (optional)
  4. We will process your request within 30 days
  5. You will receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete

⚠️ Important Information

What gets deleted:

  • Your user account and profile information
  • Email address and username
  • Phone number
  • All scam reports you submitted
  • Community posts and comments
  • App preferences and settings

What cannot be deleted:

  • Anonymized data used for analytics (no personal identifiers)
  • Data required by law to be retained
  • Backup data (will be deleted in next backup cycle, typically within 90 days)

Note: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Please ensure you want to delete your account before proceeding.

Data Deletion Timeline

  • Immediate: Account access is revoked
  • Within 24 hours: Personal data is removed from active databases
  • Within 30 days: All data is permanently deleted from our systems
  • Within 90 days: Backup data is purged

Need Help?

If you have questions about data deletion or need assistance:

  • Email: support@fraudulens.com
  • Response Time: Within 48 hours
  • In-App: Use the Help Center or Contact Support feature

Facebook Data Deletion

If you signed in using Facebook and want to delete your Facebook-connected data:

  1. Follow the account deletion steps above
  2. Your Facebook data will be automatically deleted when your account is deleted
  3. To disconnect Facebook without deleting your account, go to Settings → Connected Accounts → Disconnect Facebook

Google Data Deletion

If you signed in using Google and want to delete your Google-connected data:

  1. Follow the account deletion steps above
  2. Your Google data will be automatically deleted when your account is deleted
  3. To disconnect Google without deleting your account, go to Settings → Connected Accounts → Disconnect Google
